Delete your account and data

App: PackingList · Developer: pzhuk

You can ask us to delete your PackingList account and the data stored with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to petro.zhuk.95@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my PackingList account”.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account, so we can confirm it is yours. If you signed in with Google, use your Google account email.
  3. We will confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, and user ID).
  • All content you saved in the app and synced to your account.

Data stored only on your device is not affected. To remove it, uninstall the app.

What is kept

We do not keep any of your data after deletion, except copies in routine backups, which are removed automatically within 30 days.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
