Privacy Policy

App: PackingList · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PackingList (“the app”), provided by pzhuk, handles your information when you use the app on Android.

Using the app without an account

You can use the app without signing in. In that case, your data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Information we collect when you sign in

If you choose to sign in, the app collects and stores the following so that your data can sync across your devices:

  • Account information: your email address and a unique account ID. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Your app content: the data you create or save in the app (for example, your entries, settings, and preferences), stored under your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or other files from your device.

How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To store your data and sync it between the devices where you are signed in.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase (for example, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to handle sign-in and store your data. Firebase is provided by Google LLC and processes your information on our behalf, on servers that may be located outside your country. You can read how Google handles data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not share your information with any other third parties, except where required by law.

Data security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored using Firebase security features. Access to your stored data is restricted to your account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all data stored under it at any time by emailing us at petro.zhuk.95@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request. You can also delete data on your own device by uninstalling the app.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the email below.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, email us at petro.zhuk.95@gmail.com.
